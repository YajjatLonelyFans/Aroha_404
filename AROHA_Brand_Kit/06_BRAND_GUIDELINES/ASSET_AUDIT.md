## Decisions
- The two supplied draft SVGs were used as the vector geometry source for the new masters.
- Embedded C2PA metadata blocks and nonessential title/metadata nodes were removed from the working SVG exports to keep the deliverables clean and portable.
- All final PNG exports were rendered from the cleaned SVG masters, not enlarged from the reference PNGs.
- Monochrome variants were generated by recolouring the same vector geometry.
- App icons and favicons use the standalone symbol, not the full horizontal lockup.
- The supplied construction diagrams and master PNGs are retained unchanged in `08_SOURCE_FILES`.

## Important scope note
The supplied draft SVGs are the source geometry for this package. The construction sheets provide numerical construction intent, but no independent CAD reconstruction or automated geometric proof has been performed against every dimension. The QA report records technical checks performed on the exported files; it does not claim independent design approval.
